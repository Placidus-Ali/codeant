Steps to build Cactus Game

1. Open your Scratch
2. Choose Backdrop - Desert
3. Customize your Backdrops to have level 1 and 2, 
   Game over and Congratulation pages:
   a. click on backdrop at the top left side of the screen
   b. click convert to vector
   c. create level 1 and duplicate it to have level 1 & 2
   d. click on the empty backdrop to create 
      your Game Over and Congratulation
   e. Now you have Level 1 & 2, Game Over and Congratulation
4. Sprite
   a. choose your sprite - Dinosaur5 and place it a right 
      hand of the screen. reduce the size
   b. Upload your cactus from PC
   c. place it a the left side of the screen and reduce the
   size
5. Coding the Sprites
   a. Making the Dinosaur start at a point when the green flag is click
   b. Go to Event Block and bring "When the green flag is click"
   c. Go to Motion Block and bring "go to x: y:" (x: 200, y: -121)
   d. Go to Looks Block and bring "swutch backdrop" also bring "show" and place it under.
   e. Now you can move the Dinosaur any where and click green flag to see.
   f. Make the Dinosaur jump
   g. Go to Event Block and bring "when space key pressed"
   h. Go to Motion Block and bring 2 "glide 1 secs to x: y:" (glide 0.3, x: 200, y: 60), (glide 0.4, x: 200, y: -121) and 
      put the first positive number and second y negative to make it go up and down
   i. Make the Cactus move towards Dinosaur
   k. drag the cactus to touch the Dinosaur so as to take the position
   l. Go to Event Block and bring "when green flag clicked"
   l. Go to Control Block and bring "forever"
   m. Go to Motion Block and bring "go to x: y:" (x: -222, y: -121) 
   n. Go to Motion Block and bring "glide 1 secs to x: y:" (glide 2, x: 222, y: -121)
   o. Check if both the Dinosaur and cactus are working
   p. Now make it show Game Over when Cactus Hits Dinosaur
   q. Go to Control Block and bring "if then". place it inside the forever
   r. Go to Sensing Block and bring "touching mouse-pointer" and chnage it Dinosaur
   s. Go to Looks Block and bring "switch backgrop to level 1" and change it Game Over
   t. Go to Looks Block and bring "hide" and place it inside the "if then" to hide the cactus
   u. Go to Control Block and bring "stop all" and place it inside the "if then" to stop all the game
   v. Go to Looks Block and bring "show" and place it under "when the green flag clicked"

level 2
a. Duplicate the cactus code
b. remove the "when green flag is clicked" and "show"
c. change the time in "glide" from 2 to 1.5
d. for the Dinosuar to move to level 2, it must survive at least 9secs in level 1, and survive level 2 for 4.5 secs to get a congratulation
e. so go back to Event Block and bring back "when the green flag is clicked" and place it separate
f. Go to control Block and bring "wait 1 secs" and change it 9 secs
g. Go to Looks Block and bring "switch backdrop to level 2"
h. Go to control Block and bring "wait 1 secs" and change it to 4.5 secs
i. Go to Looks Block and bring "switch backdrop to Congratulation"
i. Go to Looks Block and bring "hide" and place it under the "switch backdrop to congratulations"
j. Go to Control Block and bring "stop all" and place it under "hide"

Make Dinosaur Hide when it shows congratulation
a. Click Dinosaur Sprite
b. Go to Control Block and bring "wait 1 secs" and change the 1 to 13.5 sec which is 9sec wait to switch to level 2 and 4.5 sec wait to switch to Congratulation
c. Go to Looks Block and bring "hide" to hide the Dinosaur when congratulation page shows
